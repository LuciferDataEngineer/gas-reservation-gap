What this means

In 2029 the outlook's busiest day is 135 TJ short even after local gas, the north-south pipe and storage. A fifth of an average 2025 day at the export plants is 780 TJ, so the volume is larger than that hole, with 644 TJ left over on paper. Without storage the same day is 1,098 TJ short, and 780 TJ does not cover it (318 TJ still missing). The published southbound pipe is 512 TJ a day and the outlook already uses it. Gas held back at Gladstone is not in Melbourne the same day. By 2030 the full-outlook hole is 257 TJ, still under a fifth of one 2025 day, with 523 TJ left over on paper.

Times are Australian Eastern Daylight Time. Gas days are the dates AEMO publishes.
