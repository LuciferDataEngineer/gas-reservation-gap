What this means

A fifth of 2025 cargoes is 267.2 PJ. The southern annual gap in the middle outlook is 1.3 PJ in 2029 and 12.2 PJ in 2030. Using that year's own LNG outlook, the fifth left over after the annual gap is 281.6 PJ in 2029 and 256.5 PJ in 2030. In 2028, home and business use plus 10% is 412.3 PJ and a fifth of outlook LNG is 271.9 PJ, so the obligation is the cap. The annual gap that year is zero. The reserved gas is much larger than the annual gap and smaller than a full year of home and business use.
