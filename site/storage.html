What this means

Iona started winter at 24.8 PJ, at or slightly above its published 24.4 PJ maximum, and ended August at 13.0 PJ. There was no empty tank in late May to park extra gas ahead of winter. By 18 November 2026 the level was 9.7 PJ, still falling after winter. Longford, the main Gippsland plant, averaged 508 TJ a day so far in 2026 against 835 in 2022. Southern maximum daily production in the outlook falls from 1,234 TJ in 2025 to 548 TJ in 2029. That decline, not a sudden jump in use, is why the peak day stops fitting.
