What this means

Once the cold, Victorian demand and Longford supply are taken into account, days when the export plants took more gas did not come with a clearly higher or lower Melbourne morning price. Prices are what the domestic markets cleared. They are not a quote for holding gas back from export, and these free files do not include an export netback.

Times are Australian Eastern Daylight Time. Gas days are the dates AEMO publishes.

1,000 terajoules = 1 petajoule. Snapshot 20 November 2026, 9:14 am AEDT. Gas flows to 18 November 2026. Prices to 31 October 2026.
